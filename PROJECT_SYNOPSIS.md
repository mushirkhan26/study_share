# PROJECT SYNOPSIS
**Institute:** Lucky Institute of Professional Studies
**Course:** BCA 6th Semester (Final Year Project)

---

### 1. Project Title
**StudyShare – A Collaborative Note-Sharing Platform**

### 2. Introduction
StudyShare is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to simplify the way students share and access academic resources. The platform allows students to upload their handwritten or digital notes, categorized by subjects and topics, making it a community-driven repository for learning materials.

### 3. Problem Statement
Currently, students share notes via unorganized methods like WhatsApp, Telegram, or physical photocopies. These methods lack searchability, categorization, and long-term accessibility. Finding specific notes for a particular unit or subject during exam time becomes a major challenge for students.

### 4. Objectives of the Project
* To provide a centralized platform for all academic study materials.
* To implement a robust search and filter system (by subject, author, or topic).
* To enable students to contribute to their peer community by uploading high-quality notes.
* To provide a clean and intuitive UI for seamless navigation.

### 5. Tools & Technologies
* **Frontend:** Flutter (Dart)
* **Backend:** Node.js / Express.js
* **Database:** MongoDB
* **State Management:** Provider / Riverpod (as per implementation)
* **Storage:** Cloudinary / Firebase Storage (for PDF/Images)

### 6. Proposed System / Working
The application follows a Client-Server architecture:
1. **User Authentication:** Students sign up/login using their credentials.
2. **Dashboard:** Users can see trending notes and categories.
3. **Search & Filter:** A dedicated search screen allows users to filter notes by subjects like Java, Python, DBMS, DSA, etc.
4. **Upload Module:** Verified users can upload files with metadata (Subject Name, Unit Number, Topic).
5. **View/Download:** Users can view the notes within the app or download them for offline use.

### 7. Expected Outcome
The final product will be a functional Android/iOS application that serves as a digital library for students, promoting peer-to-peer learning and ensuring that quality study material is accessible to everyone at any time.
